# Publication figure definition and caption

## Mathematical definition

Let

\[
\gamma:[0,L]\longrightarrow\mathbb R^3,
\qquad
\gamma(s)=(x(s),y(s),z(s))
\]

denote the recorded incoming Path 22 segment parameterized by cumulative
arclength, and put \(\sigma=s/L\). Define the horizontal endpoint direction

\[
e=\frac{P_{xy}\gamma(L)-P_{xy}\gamma(0)}
        {\lVert P_{xy}\gamma(L)-P_{xy}\gamma(0)\rVert},
\qquad n=(-e_2,e_1),
\]

and its chord-normal displacement

\[
\eta(s)=n\mathbin{\cdot}
\bigl(P_{xy}\gamma(s)-P_{xy}\gamma(0)\bigr).
\]

The ambient coordinate planes are copies of \(\mathbb R^2\). The available
record determines the finite restriction \(\gamma|_{[0,L]}\). A global smooth
curve on the truly unbounded plane would require a proved extension
\(\widetilde\gamma:\mathbb R\to\mathbb R^3\); the figure does not assign values
outside the recorded interval.

## Figure caption

**Figure X. Ordered projections of the incoming Path 22 streamline.**
**(a)** Horizontal projection \(P_{xy}\gamma(\sigma)\); arrows indicate
increasing normalized arclength. **(b)** Vertical coordinate \(z(\sigma)\).
**(c)** Horizontal chord-normal displacement \(\eta(\sigma)\). Dashed curves
in (b) and (c) are the endpoint chords. Symbols identify the backward-traced
measured-field edge, the original \((80,20,6)\) seed, and the previously
declared recurrent-channel entry. The solid curves show the exact ordered
coordinates without smoothing; \(x\), \(y\), and \(z\) are reported in the
source dataset's undocumented field units. The projections expose a broad incoming
counter-turn followed by an increasingly rapid transverse oscillation toward
the recurrent channel.
