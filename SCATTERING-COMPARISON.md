# Path 22 scattering comparison

The canonical modular primitive-cycle identity was rejected before visual
comparison: determinant-normalized Path 22 ensemble monodromies have traces
near `2.096`, while a hyperbolic `PSL(2,Z)` primitive geodesic has integer trace
of magnitude at least 3.

The surviving candidate treated the positive and negative Poincaré branches
as incoming and outgoing scattering sectors. In Euclidean coordinates, inverse,
adjoint, and unitarity residuals are large.

A symmetric bilinear form `G` was then fit on four spatial residue classes
under the frozen condition

```text
N_negative^T G N_positive = G.
```

Every fold produces one positive and one negative metric eigenvalue, suggesting
a local pseudo-unitary or Lorentzian form. The form does not generalize:
held-out residuals are `0.110`, `0.191`, `0.079`, `0.931`, and `1.006`.

The current affine branch model therefore fails the canonical scattering gate.
The stable indefinite signature remains an exploratory observation, not an RH
representation or an invitation to tune coordinates.
